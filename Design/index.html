Nansha, ChinaCompleted

The Greater Bay Area Sports Centre in Nansha

A 60,000-seat stadium, 20,000-seat indoor arena, and aquatics centre form a sports campus within a 70-hectare riverside park. Layered roofs recall traditional Chinese fans, combining shade and rain protection with natural ventilation inspired by Lingnan architecture. Curved facades channel summer breezes, while landscaped recreation areas and flood-managing wetlands connect major sporting events with everyday community use.

Hangzhou, China2022–TBC

Hangzhou International Sports Centre

A 60,000-seat football stadium, 19,000-seat indoor arena, and aquatics centre are linked by a terraced podium inspired by Hangzhou's hillside tea farms. Open, louvred facades connect spectator terraces with panoramic city views. The compact arrangement reserves almost half the site for public space, while solar-informed shading, hybrid ventilation, photovoltaics, and riverbank wetlands integrate environmental performance with a new riverfront park.

Shenzhen, ChinaCompleted

Shenzhen Linhai Shuangjie River Bridge

Part of a 243.6-metre, six-lane arterial road project, this approximately 128-metre bridge integrates a sculptural steel arch with the waterfront landscape. Its arch-and-girder system uses flexible hangers, while the curved steel box girder was installed by incremental launching, a demanding construction process. Work began in April 2023 and was completed ahead of schedule after 21 months.

Virtual environment2022

Metrotopia Metaverse

Created with ArchAgenda, Metrotopia translates the spatial qualities of a city into a shared virtual environment for the global design community. Built in Unreal Engine 5, it combines interconnected exhibition spaces, spatial audio, and real-time multi-user interaction. Its opening exhibition, Knowledge Transfer, presented work by international architecture studios, positioning the platform as a place for displaying projects, exchanging ideas, and collaboration.

The New World Exhibition

Presented at Beijing's Guardian Art Centre, ten thematic zones trace 15 years of Zaha Hadid Architects' work in China, from the Guangzhou Opera House to Leeza SOHO and Daxing International Airport. Built landmarks and 25 projects in development sit alongside research in robotics, 3D printing, artificial intelligence, and virtual architecture, connecting the studio's design evolution with new approaches to materials, construction, and environmental performance.

Guangzhou, ChinaSeptember 2019Winning proposal

International Urban Design Competition for Baiyun Lake Digital Technology City

A 28-square-kilometre urban design study structured around Baiyun Lake as one central core and four surrounding districts. Connecting the Pearl River water system with green corridors from Baiyun Mountain, the proposal brings Guangzhou's mountain-and-water landscape into a new technology district. Its design draws on Lingnan traditions of living with nature to unite innovation, ecology, and urban life.

Joint design team: Guangzhou Urban Planning & Design Survey Research Institute, AECOM, and ISA Internationales Stadtbauatelier. My contribution: ISA design team member.
