Conference presentations, academic recognition, and university life.

Conference8–10 July 2026Lisbon, Portugal

Oral presentation at 2nd International Conference on Zero Carbon Built Environment

Presented an occupancy-based control strategy for BIPV smart windows in Hong Kong residential retrofits. The study combines EnergyPlus, Radiance, and photovoltaic modeling to balance energy use, thermal comfort, and daylight. Evaluation under a projected 2050 climate explores whether controls optimized for today can continue to support effective building operation in a warmer future.

Conference24–27 May 2026Beijing, China

Oral presentation at 20th National Environmental Conference for Doctoral Students

Delivered an oral presentation on surrogate-assisted, daily rolling HVAC setpoint optimization for BIPV-integrated office buildings. The work explores how cooling schedules can coordinate energy demand with on-site solar generation to reduce carbon emissions and grid stress. Held at Tsinghua University, the conference was organized in conjunction with the 2026 Asian Universities Alliance Postgraduate Academic Forum.

AwardJanuary 2026Elsevier

Top Cited Paper Award in 2025, Materials Today Energy

Our review of machine learning applications in perovskite solar cells received the Materials Today Energy Top Cited Paper Award. The paper traces data-driven methods from materials discovery and device performance to process optimization and system integration. It brings these stages together to examine opportunities for addressing persistent challenges in stability, efficiency, and scalable production.

Conference5–7 September 2025Shanghai, China

Oral presentation at 2nd International Conference on Carbon Neutrality

Presented a multi-stage, cross-climate analysis of design variables affecting life-cycle carbon in BIPV-integrated buildings. The work connects simulation and sensitivity analysis to identify influential decisions in building form, envelope design, and photovoltaic configuration. The Shanghai conference brought together research on renewable energy, building decarbonization, and other pathways toward carbon neutrality.
