Research article26 July 2026Published onlineEnergy and Buildings

Surrogate-assisted rolling-horizon multi-objective HVAC setpoint optimization for office buildings with BIPV systems

Ling Mao, Kailang Wu, Changying Xiang

A surrogate-assisted workflow optimizes daily cooling setpoints in BIPV-equipped offices by coordinating demand with solar generation, tariffs, and grid peaks. EnergyPlus re-simulation verifies the selected annual schedules. The results show how pre-cooling and demand shifting can reduce energy use, operating costs, and peak imports while maintaining comfort and improving photovoltaic self-consumption.

Research article12 May 2026Published onlineBuilding and Environment

Sketch2Radiation: Predicting annual solar radiation maps of buildings from architectural sketches with depth and camera pose

Ling Mao, Changying Xiang

Sketch2Radiation predicts annual solar radiation on visible building surfaces from a digitally generated sketch, depth map, camera pose, and climate information. Using a dataset of 480,000 simulated samples across 1,000 buildings and 20 cities, it provides rapid, view-aligned feedback. Average solar-potential prediction errors below 1% support preliminary photovoltaic placement before detailed simulation.

Research article14 January 2026Published onlineEnergy and Buildings

A cross-climate multi-stage approach to life-cycle carbon sensitivity analysis in BIPV-integrated building design

Ling Mao, Changying Xiang

This study combines building simulation, life-cycle assessment, and interpretable machine learning to examine 6,000 design cases across six climates. It identifies how climate, BIPV configuration, building form, and envelope choices shape carbon performance at different design stages. Multi-objective optimization then reveals climate-adaptive design ranges that balance life-cycle carbon, daylight availability, and glare.

Research article29 April 2026Published onlineApplied Energy

Adaptive co-planning of building-integrated photovoltaics and vehicle-to-grid infrastructure under phase-out policy incentives: A two-stage multi-objective optimization framework

Kailang Wu, Ling Mao, Changying Xiang

A two-stage framework coordinates BIPV capacity and vehicle-to-grid infrastructure as feed-in tariffs are phased out. A Hong Kong case study shows how operations shift from tariff-driven arbitrage to matching demand with solar generation. Joint planning reduces life-cycle cost, carbon emissions, and load variability, while phased deployment helps manage early investment risk.

Review article21 March 2025Published onlineMaterials Today Energy

Structural colors for building-integrated photovoltaics (BIPV): Multidisciplinary perspectives towards sustainable design

Ling Mao, Changying Xiang

Drawing on 131 studies, this review examines how structural colors can reconcile photovoltaic performance with architectural appearance. It connects optical design, color evaluation, durability, manufacturing, and life-cycle sustainability. The analysis identifies efficiency trade-offs, scaling challenges, and inconsistent standards, outlining priorities for bringing visually adaptable BIPV technologies into wider architectural use.

Review article15 November 2024Published onlineMaterials Today Energy

A comprehensive review of machine learning applications in perovskite solar cells: Materials discovery, device performance, process optimization and systems integration

Ling Mao, Changying Xiang

This review maps machine learning across the perovskite solar-cell development process: materials discovery, device performance, process optimization, and system integration. Synthesizing 119 research papers and 29 reviews, it examines how data-driven methods can accelerate progress beyond trial-and-error experimentation. Persistent challenges in stability, efficiency, and scalable manufacturing define the next research priorities.
