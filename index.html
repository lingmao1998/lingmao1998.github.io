I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Integrative Systems and Design at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. My research connects building performance simulation, AI-driven surrogate modeling, and photovoltaic systems to support climate-resilient, low-carbon building design.

Research interests

  • Building performance simulation
  • AI-driven surrogate modeling
  • Building-integrated photovoltaics (BIPV)
  • Climate-resilient buildings
  • Life-cycle carbon assessment

Surrogate-assisted rolling-horizon multi-objective HVAC setpoint optimization for office buildings with BIPV systems

Daily cooling schedules coordinate energy demand with solar generation, electricity tariffs, and grid peaks. Surrogate models and EnergyPlus re-simulation identify strategies that reduce energy use, costs, and peak imports while maintaining comfort.

Energy and Buildings26 July 2026

Sketch2Radiation: Predicting annual solar radiation maps of buildings from architectural sketches with depth and camera pose

Sketch2Radiation predicts annual facade solar radiation from digitally generated sketches, depth maps, and camera poses. A multi-climate dataset supports rapid, view-aligned feedback for early-stage building design and photovoltaic placement.

Building and Environment12 May 2026

A cross-climate multi-stage approach to life-cycle carbon sensitivity analysis in BIPV-integrated building design

Simulation, life-cycle assessment, and interpretable machine learning reveal how climate and design choices shape building carbon performance. Analysis of 6,000 cases identifies climate-adaptive strategies balancing carbon emissions, daylight, and glare.

Energy and Buildings14 January 2026
